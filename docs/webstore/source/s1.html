Kilogent Browser

Your agents, in the sites
you’re already signed in to.

Lend this Chrome to your Kilogent workspace. Agents open their own tabs and work in your logged-in tools — without ever seeing a password.

  1. Sign in with Kilogent. Approve this browser once, in your workspace.
  2. Offer it to your workspaces. Tick the ones that may ask to use it.
  3. Share and permit in Kilogent. You choose which agents, and for how long.
